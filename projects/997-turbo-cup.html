997 Turbo CUP

A full 997.1 Turbo to 997.2 CUP conversion, built the BBi way.

Overview

A full 997.1 Turbo to 997.2 CUP conversion, recently completed at BBi Autosport. This build involved much more than a visual transformation: nearly every major component was reworked or adapted, including CUP fender flares, front uprights, bumpers, decklid, cooling system, brakes, suspension arms and rear wing.

A significant amount of engineering went into maximizing ground clearance without compromising performance. One of the biggest challenges was packaging the turbo system as tightly as possible beneath the car. Turbo systems on these cars are notorious for clearance issues under maximum suspension compression, so every millimeter mattered.

To accomplish this, I designed the exhaust manifolds to be 3D-printed in Inconel, keeping equal-length runners while tucking the headers extremely tight to the underside of the chassis. The charge piping was 3D-printed in aluminum, with an ovalized center section that maximizes ground clearance without sacrificing flow. The intercooler outlet ducting was custom-designed to integrate with the CUP bumper, printed in multiple PET-CF sections and bonded together to achieve the required geometry.

Beyond the major components, nearly every detail was custom-engineered for this application, including the exhaust system, wastegate dumps, turbo mounting hardware for a pair of 76 mm Xona turbos, front turn signal mounts and countless other one-off parts. The end result is a 997 Turbo with full CUP-spec bodywork and a thoroughly custom-engineered twin-turbo setup.

Scope

  • Equal-length exhaust manifolds, 3D-printed in Inconel
  • Aluminum 3D-printed charge piping with ovalized center section
  • PET-CF intercooler outlet ducting integrated with the CUP bumper
  • Exhaust system, wastegate dumps and turbo mounting hardware
  • CUP bodywork, cooling, brakes and suspension adaptation
