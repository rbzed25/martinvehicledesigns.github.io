Overview

In 2025 BBi Autosport made a big push to conquer Pikes Peak, the famous hill-climb race in Colorado. As the lead engineer at BBi, I was tasked with turning a 992 Porsche race car into a time-attack monster.

To do this, I created a twin-turbo system for the originally naturally aspirated engine. Everything from the intake manifold to the exhaust manifold was redesigned with performance in mind. Aside from the twin-turbo assembly, I also designed a fully ducted center radiator to improve aerodynamics and cooling, and added front wheel ducting to evacuate turbulent air from the wheel wells.

SolidWorks and ANSYS were used for CAD modeling and aerodynamic studies. As project lead, I was also in charge of part organization and installation.

Scope

  • Twin-turbo conversion of a naturally aspirated engine
  • Intake and exhaust manifold redesign
  • Fully ducted center radiator for cooling and aero
  • Front wheel-well ducting
  • Part organization and installation

Result

10th overall · 3rd in class
